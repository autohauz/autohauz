# AUTOHAUZ_AU_SEO_DASHBOARD_SPEC

## Metrics to Track
Once the site is deployed and Search Console is verified, the following KPIs should form the SEO dashboard:

### 1. Organic Search Metrics (Google Search Console)
- **Clicks & Impressions**: Track growth over 3-month rolling windows.
- **Indexation Status**: Monitor the "Pages" report. Ensure the ratio of indexed VDPs matches the active inventory count. Monitor the "Excluded by 'noindex' tag" report to confirm sold vehicles are accurately categorized.
- **Rich Results**: Monitor the "Merchant Listings", "Product Snippets", and "Breadcrumbs" enhancements reports for schema warnings.

### 2. Local SEO Metrics (Google Business Profile)
- **Profile Views & Interactions**: Track direction requests and phone calls.
- **Website Clicks**: Measure traffic originating from the GBP.

### 3. Business Conversion Metrics (Analytics)
- Organic sessions resulting in:
  - Finance form submissions.
  - Test drive bookings.
  - Trade-in valuations.
  - WhatsApp/Phone clicks.
